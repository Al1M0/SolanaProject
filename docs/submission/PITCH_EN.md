## 0:00–0:20 · The problem

A Solana researcher sees several successful wallets buying the same token. Should they follow? Wallet activity alone cannot answer that. The useful question is whether this idea beats a simple baseline after fees and delays on a separate period.

## 0:20–0:45 · The product

Solana Quant Research Lab turns that question into an audit. State a hypothesis, select the wallets and tokens, check data quality, and inspect training. Then freeze the configuration and evaluate a historical holdout against buy-and-hold and a fixed momentum rule, using the same execution engine.

## 0:45–1:10 · What the user learns

We show costs, exposure, turnover and individual trades. Our clearly labeled synthetic demonstration is unchanged: the strategy makes about one point three percent before costs, but loses one point nine percent after costs. Buy-and-hold does better. Rejecting a weak hypothesis is a useful research outcome.

## 1:10–1:35 · Evidence and limits

Every attempt stays visible. A frozen manifest binds the data, assumptions and engine. The export lets another researcher reproduce the results. Historical data may already be known to the user, so the lock does not prove an unseen holdout or profitable alpha.
