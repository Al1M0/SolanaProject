# Source-linked comparison

Primary documentation checked 2026-10-03. This is a comparison of documented capabilities and our current implementation, not an exhaustive competitive audit. A feature absent from a referenced page is **unknown**, not absent from the product. We have not purchased or tested competitor accounts.

| Tool | Capability verified from primary source | Relationship to this audit workflow | Remaining verification |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Nansen Profiler](https://docs.nansen.ai/api/profiler/address-pnl-and-trade-performance) | Address PnL/trade-performance endpoints expose realized PnL, returns, win rate and token statistics; Solana is in the supported chain enum. | Strong wallet evidence/discovery alternative and potential data input. Our prototype centers a user-defined hypothesis, shared-engine baselines, configuration locking and an exportable research trail. | Nansen's complete experiment/backtesting workflow, current account access, licensing and comparable study costs are not verified. No claim it lacks benchmarks or reproducibility. |
| [GMGN](https://docs.gmgn.ai/index/track-smart-money) | Documents following smart money/KOL activity on Solana and real-time notifications, with a copy-trading orientation. | Supports wallet discovery and monitoring. Our intended decision is whether historical evidence survives costs and a baseline before committing to an idea. | Complete research/backtesting/export capabilities and account pricing remain unverified. |
| [Dune](https://docs.dune.com/data-catalog/solana/overview) | Solana raw and decoded tables cover transactions, instructions and account balance changes for research. | A flexible SQL research alternative and possible future data adapter. Researchers can build their own audits on it. | This prototype does not integrate Dune, and we have not benchmarked user effort or query cost. |
| [VectorBT](https://vectorbt.dev/) | Official project presents Python quantitative analysis and backtesting with portfolio simulation and parameter exploration. | A powerful notebook-based alternative. Our proposed advantage is a guided Solana evidence workflow, not a stronger general backtesting engine. | No superiority claim; feature-by-feature or performance comparison has not been conducted. |
| This prototype | Training and holdout comparisons, immutable manifests, training-only sensitivity, explicit costs/gaps, trade evidence and offline rerun ZIP. | Narrow workflow for independent researchers; actual benefits require interviews/usability evidence. | REAL study, signed-wallet interaction, browser manual QA and demand evidence still need verification. |

A prospective differentiation test: ask five users to review the same hypothesis using their current tools and the Lab. Record comprehension, missing-data detection, manual steps and willingness to use it again. Do not turn five exploratory sessions into a market-size or statistical claim.
