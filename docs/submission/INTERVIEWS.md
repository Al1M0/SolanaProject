# Five prospective-user interviews and usability task

Status: not recruited or conducted. The human team will recruit five independent Solana researchers who have recently checked a wallet-based idea manually. Obtain consent for notes/recording; use participant codes rather than personal identifiers in public exports.

## Interview guide — 20 minutes each

1. Tell us about the last wallet idea you investigated. What decision were you trying to make?
2. Walk us through your actual tools, data sources and manual steps. Ask to see a redacted example if comfortable.
3. Where do missing prices, wallet inventory, execution costs or token selection change your conclusion?
4. How do you choose a comparison baseline and a separate evaluation period today?
5. What makes you trust or reject a research result? What would make this workflow unusable?
6. After the task, what felt useful, confusing or unnecessary? Ask for examples, not general praise.
7. What do you currently spend on research tools? Would per-study or monthly pricing fit your usage? Proposed prices are assumptions; do not ask leading “would you pay $19?” questions before exploring alternatives.
8. What would you need before using this on a real decision? Would you volunteer another hypothesis for a follow-up? Record the answer without treating it as a purchase.

## Usability task — no coaching, 8–10 minutes

“Assess whether the supplied wallet hypothesis outperforms a reasonable baseline after costs on the separate period. Explain the evidence and export enough material for another researcher to reproduce it.”

Use the untuned seed-33 SYNTHETIC study until genuine provider data is available. Tell the participant it is synthetic. Ask them to select/inspect quality, inspect training, understand the seven-row sensitivity grid, freeze, evaluate holdout, compare both baselines, inspect one trade/cost breakdown and export the ZIP.

Observe whether they can distinguish REAL/SYNTHETIC, gross/net, training/holdout and the meaning of a lock. Ask them to explain why positive gross returns are not proof of alpha. Show an incomplete-data fixture separately and ask whether performance should be shown.

Record elapsed time, completion, help required, wrong interpretation, strongest pain point and desired change. Pre-specified exploratory success criteria: 4/5 complete the core audit and 5/5 recognize the synthetic label and absence of proven alpha. These are usability targets, not statistical confidence or measured outcomes.

Compare with their current workflow only when they can demonstrate it. Keep genuine quotes verbatim with consent and evidence references. Leave every result empty until a session happens. Use FEEDBACK_LOG.md.
