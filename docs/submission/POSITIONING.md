# Positioning

Solana Quant Research Lab helps independent Solana researchers audit a wallet-based trading hypothesis against transparent baselines after modeled costs, then freeze and export the evidence for reproducible review.

**Initial user:** an independent researcher who manually checks wallet ideas in an explorer, wallet analytics, spreadsheets and Python notebooks. This persona is a product hypothesis, not evidence of interviewed customers.

**Current alternative:** discover wallets in Nansen/GMGN; check transactions and market history; join data manually; write a notebook; maintain assumptions and results in separate files.

**Proposed advantage:** one inspectable question from dataset quality to training, frozen configuration, historical holdout, benchmarks, trade evidence and an offline reproduction bundle. Faster and clearer review is an unvalidated usability hypothesis. We do not claim competitors lack these capabilities.

**Scope:** one long-only accumulation strategy, two simple benchmarks, small studies and one execution model. Wallets, market charts and live alerts support the audit. No automated trading, new token, NFT or investment recommendation.

**Evidence today:** implemented software and regression checks; one untuned SYNTHETIC engineering study; an actual fixed 48-hour wallet ingestion check with recorded exclusions and five independent public-RPC swap comparisons. REAL performance is blocked by denied historical-liquidity access and missing prices, rather than missing server keys. Human explorer review remains pending. No genuine user interviews, revenue, partnerships or market-size estimate have been recorded.

**Two-person priorities:** one student owns authorized data acquisition, reconciliation and research limitations; the other owns usability testing, UI repairs and demo recording. These are proposed roles, not a claim about work already performed.
